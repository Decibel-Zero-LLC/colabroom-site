Why anybody bothers

    
The singer drops it a tone and the chart doesn't change.

    

      The singer changes key; 1, 4 and 5 still mean the same places in the song. Each player reads them in the new key.
    

  


  

    
How to read one

    
Count the chords of the key, one to seven.

    

      In G, C is 4 and D is 5. Minor chords get a dash, so Am is 2-. A chord outside the key gets a sharp or flat: F is ♭7. Roman numerals use upper- and lowercase to show chord quality.
    

  


  

    
Minor songs

    
There are two right answers.

    

      For a minor song, you can count from the relative major (A minor is 6- in C) or from the minor tonic (A minor is 1-). Choose the convention your band uses. Roman numerals count from the minor tonic.
