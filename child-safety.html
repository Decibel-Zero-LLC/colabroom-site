Child safety

Last updated October 25, 2026

CoLabRoom is published by Decibel Zero LLC. Child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming and sexual content involving children are forbidden here. This page explains our age rules and how to tell us that a child may be at risk.

If a child needs help now

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 in the United States, or your local emergency number. In Florida, then call the Florida Abuse Hotline (1-800-96-ABUSE) — 1-800-962-2873. The Florida Department of Children and Families explains how to report suspected abuse, abandonment or neglect.

A report to CoLabRoom does not contact emergency services or the hotline. Do not wait for an answer from us when a child needs urgent help.

Who may use CoLabRoom

CoLabRoom is for musicians 13 and over. People aged 13 to 17 may work on music in ordinary rooms with people they know. Read the age rules in our Terms with a parent or guardian.

From 13, musicians can use their own music and ordinary rooms, take ownership of an ordinary room they are already in, and ask Clef about their own songs when Anthropic answers. Clef is an AI guide, not a person; with OpenAI it stays for adults, and it is not on in lesson rooms.

Calls in ordinary rooms open from 13. One adult and one person under 18 cannot call alone: if a call drops to those two, the under-18 person's sound and video wait until a third person joins. Older beta builds that cannot keep this protection do not admit under-18s to calls.

When someone under 18 joins by email or code from an adult they do not already work with, we ask whether they know them in real life. One-to-one messages and calls between them stay closed for seven days after that join; the room thread works. The call protection above still applies after that week. Only join rooms with people you know and trust.

Meeting new people, public listings, lesson links, lesson rooms and lesson records stay for people 18 and over. So do taking ownership of a lesson or class room, keeping or taking ownership of a room made at a door, leading a team, and keeping a stage as a sound tech. The Terms explain all adult features.

These rules describe newer builds now in testing. Older beta builds may keep some of these features closed. An account that has not answered the age question must do so before recording, uploading, messaging, making or joining a room or song, or inviting somebody.

CoLabRoom does not choose, check, employ or recommend teachers. Lessons stay for adults until a parent or guardian can invite the teacher and see and join the lesson. CoLabRoom is not offered to schools as a school service, and children under 13 may not use it through a school.

The under-13 rule

A child under 13 may not have an account. Our Terms require us to stop an account working as soon as we learn it belongs to a child under 13, and to delete the account and what the child put on CoLabRoom within 14 days, including their takes and words in other people’s rooms. Backups follow the Privacy Policy.

If you believe your child has an account, or want their information deleted, write to support@colabroom.com. Tell us enough to find the account. Do not send identity documents unless we ask for what is needed to handle your request.

Tell us about a concern

In the app, open the profile, song or recording, choose Report, and choose A child may be in danger when it is offered. On an older version, choose Something else and explain the concern. You can also write to hotline@colabroom.com or abuse@colabroom.com.

Say where we can find the account or material and why you are concerned. Do not download, attach or forward suspected child sexual abuse material. Tell us where it is instead. You do not need to collect evidence yourself.

Child-safety reports need a person’s urgent attention. We review the concern, restrict or remove material or accounts where appropriate, and report to the authorities where the law requires it. Reporting a concern does not itself decide that someone has done something wrong.

Reporting sexual exploitation

You can report suspected online child sexual exploitation to the NCMEC CyberTipline. The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children explains what it receives and how it handles a report.

When we learn of apparent child sexual abuse material or other child sexual exploitation that we are required to report, we report what we have to NCMEC and preserve the report and associated material as the law requires. A person handles that report; pressing Report in CoLabRoom does not automatically file with NCMEC. The Florida hotline is a separate route for suspected child abuse.
