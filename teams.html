Make this a team.

In a band room, the owner taps Make this a team. A team has a leader and a chair for every part.

The leader names the chairs: Keys, Bass, Vocal 2. Leading a team is for people 18 and over.

2:00 pm · Before the night

The sound tech gets the gig first.

Send the advance: the set order with each song's key and tempo, a stage plot, and an input list by chair.

No account needed. The tech can send corrections to the input list and plot before the show. The link stops working 48 hours after the show.

The names file for the desk carries channel names, colours and icons only. Not yet proven on a real desk.

A tech who has an account can keep the stage, from 18.

9:40 pm · On the night

The leader calls the next one.

Before the set: Keep the set for the stage on the leader's phone, Follow the stage on everyone else's.

The leader calls a song and a key. Every following stand turns to it, in its own reading. Bluetooth, phone to phone. No signal needed.

11:15 pm · Record the night

The night, cut into songs.

One phone on the mic stand records the set and cuts it into songs on the phone. Pin a moment as it happens. The screen must stay on.

Keep the night as a folder saves one WAV per song, from bar 1, lined up for Logic.

Record the night from your pocket stays private until the leader claims it and every player says yes. One no keeps it private.
