Calls

When you are free at the same time.

Audio or video, with the whole room or a room of just two. Put up a song: chords and words fill the screen, faces along the top. The call itself is never recorded.

On the call's Stage: Record a pass, Hear the band, Take turns.

Calls are for the people in your room, from 13. For now, a room of just one adult and someone under 18 has no call, and a call that drops to just those two waits for a third person.

Your beta build may differ. See the current child-safety information. Recording a pass has its own recording and consent rules.

After the call

The room keeps a note.

After a call: who was there, roughly how long, the takes added and whose words changed. Anyone on the call can add a line.

It is not a recording, and only the people who were there can read it.
