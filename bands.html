Gigs

Talk

The band talks in the room.

Every room is a thread. Say something and the band hears about it; write to one person and only they do. Messages is the one place for all of it — your people, who is here right now, and every room you are in.

Calls

When you are free at the same time.

Call the whole room, or one person in a room of just the two of you. Audio and video. Put a song up and its chords and words take the screen, with the faces along the top. The call itself is never recorded. Calls are for adults, 18 and over.

After the call

The room keeps a note.

When a call ends, the room keeps a note: who was there, roughly how long, the takes added and whose words changed. Anybody who was on the call can add a line.

It is not a recording, and only the people who were there can read it.
