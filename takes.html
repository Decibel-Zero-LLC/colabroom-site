Nobody gets overwritten

Mute anything. It only goes quiet for you.

Try the song without the harmony. Their take stays; only your mix changes.

  • Your takes are yours. Mute one and it only goes quiet for you.
  • Everyone else mutes. Which is what they wanted anyway.
  • Nothing collides. Two people recording at once both land.
Sideways

Turn the phone. It becomes a desk.

Turn sideways to line up the takes as faders. Hear the balance as you adjust it.

In time

Your take lands where you played it.

Every phone has its own delay. Once the song has a sheet, your take is pulled onto the beat where you played it.

Yours

Take it with you.

On a phone, open Save in the song's Takes. The mix saves the song and every audible take as one WAV.

Every take, separately saves one file per take, lined up and at tempo for your DAW. Yours to keep.
