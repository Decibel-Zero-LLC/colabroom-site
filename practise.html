Hear me play

Play it, and it listens.

On a phone, press Hear me play in Perform. It listens to your chords. Nothing is recorded.

Doubted chords get surer as you play. Play another chord twice and it offers that one first. Nothing is rewritten without a tap.

It says so itself: I can only hear you with the recording off. On phones, not in the browser.
