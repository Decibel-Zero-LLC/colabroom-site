The Open Mic

Meet somebody
you haven't met.

A room is yours and your band's. The Open Mic is the one place you step onto on purpose — and step off again whenever you like.

Off until you say

Nothing here is public because you made it.

Being findable is a switch, and it starts off. Your songs stay in your room until you put one up. Three decisions make something public. Each one is yours, and undoable.

The Open Mic is for adults. Under 18, the rooms you are invited into work as they always do.

Asks

Say what the song needs.

Put a song up and name what it's missing — bass on the second verse. The ask carries the song with it: the audio, the key, the tempo, what's on it already. Answering is one tap.

Listen

You decide by ear, in about ten seconds.

"Guitarist" doesn't tell you metal from jazz. Pick what you play, press Listen, hear the songs that need it. The rest you judge by ear.

Open Mic Night

Once a week, a night.

The Open Mic has a night every week, and the app shows when in your own time zone. Put a song that is already on the Open Mic on the night's list. The list is in the order songs went on it, and never in any other. Press Remind me and the app tells you before a night with songs on its list.

Only an adult who owns the song's room can put it on the list. Nothing is counted, and there is no audience number.

Open the door

Up for company?

Working on a song and wouldn't mind somebody dropping in? Open your door. Your people see it lit, and one tap knocks. Let them in and you get a room for the two of you, where they hear that one song and you can talk it over.

Everybody else on the Open Mic sees it only if you say so. It closes by itself, and nobody is told a knock went unanswered.

No ladder

Nobody is ranked.

No badges, no levels, no streaks, no chart of the most liked. The moment a room ranks people it stops being a room. A phone recording gets in as readily as a mastered mix.

Safe to accept

Report and block, on everything.

Anywhere somebody else's music, picture or message appears, you can report it and block them. Blocking closes anything already open between you. Nothing from a stranger is reviewed before you see it, and the app says so.

An honest empty

A quiet noticeboard is a healthy one.

A noticeboard, not a feed. Nothing scrolls forever. Some days nobody needs anything — that's a small room, not a broken one.
