Deleting your account

To delete your account

  1. Open CoLabRoom and go to the Account screen.
  2. Open Sign-in and phone (Account & device in older versions), then choose Delete account.
  3. If other people are in a room of yours, it is listed first. Settle each one: Hand it over to somebody in it, or Close the room.
  4. Confirm.

This is immediate and permanent, with no undo and no recovery window. It removes your profile, your words, and every room nobody else is in, with its songs.

Songs you started and takes you shared in other people's rooms stay with their bands, without your name. Records and files needed for legal or safety preservation may be retained. See our Privacy policy and Terms.

If you cannot sign in and need your account removed, email support@colabroom.com from the address you signed up with.
