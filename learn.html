Ask Clef

A guide for your song.

Clef is a guide in the app, not a person. Ask about your own song’s key, chords, shapes or what might fit. Type or speak.

It does not hear you play. It reads what the app worked out about your song, and says when that is not certain. Its answers come from an AI model and from the app's own music theory. From 13 with Anthropic; adults only with OpenAI. Age rules →

Lessons

One link. A room for each of you.

On Learn, Teach with a link gives each student a private room with the teacher. Have a code? Tap Use a teacher's code. Choose a song; Lesson work keeps What to practise and Your work beside it.

The teacher leaves a passage, a speed and what to work on in their own words. Practise opens it at that speed when the sheet and recording support it; otherwise it opens the whole song.

Your take stays private until you share it. In a two-person lesson, Your work offers Send to your teacher by name. If more people are in the room, sharing is with the room, and the app says so.

No grades, no streaks. The teacher never sees whether you opened what they left, only what you choose to send. Clef is off in lesson rooms: ask the teacher. Lessons are for adults: both of you are 18 or over.
