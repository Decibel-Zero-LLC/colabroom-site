Ask Clef

A teacher that knows your key.

Clef is the AI teacher in the app. Ask it about one of your own songs: the key, the chords, the shapes, what might fit. Type the question, or say it.

It does not hear you play. It reads what the app worked out about your song, and when it would only be guessing, it says so.

It shows you how

It never writes the song for you.

Ask for the notes that fit and you get them. The choosing is yours. It will not write you a line, a melody, a harmony or the words.

Or tell it what to do: loop the chorus, slower, show me G. Clef is for adults, 18 and over.

Lessons

One link. A room for each of you.

A teacher makes one link. Everybody who opens it gets a private room with the teacher. The teacher leaves a song and what to practise, and the takes come back to the same room.

No grades, no streaks. The teacher never sees whether you opened what they left, only what you choose to send. In a lesson room the questions go to the teacher, not the app. Lessons are for adults: both of you are 18 or over.
